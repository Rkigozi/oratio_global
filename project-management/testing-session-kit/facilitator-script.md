# Facilitator Script — Oratio In-Person Testing Session

**Duration:** ~45 min
**Testers:** 4-5 (you + them in a room)
**Format:** Think-aloud — everyone narrates while using the app on their own phone
**Recording:** Voice record the debrief (confirm consent at start)

---

## Pre-Session Checklist (do before testers arrive)

- [ ] Print tester task cards (1 per person + 1 spare = 6 copies)
- [ ] Print observation checklists (1 per person = 5-6 copies)
- [ ] Print 1 debrief capture sheet
- [ ] Stick QR code onto each task card (or print directly)
- [ ] Test the QR code scans to the correct Netlify URL
- [ ] Test voice recorder — make sure mic picks up a room full of people
- [ ] Set up seating — everyone in a circle or around a table
- [ ] Confirm wifi works / mobile data is sufficient
- [ ] Have a pen ready

---

## 1. Arrival & Setup (0:00–0:05)

- [ ] Testers arrive, settle in
- [ ] Confirm everyone has a charged phone with internet
- [ ] Start voice recorder

- [ ] Hand out tester task card
- [ ] Say:

> "Thanks for being here. I'm testing a prototype prayer app called Oratio. There are no right or wrong answers — I'm testing the app, not you.
>
> Here's how the next 45 minutes will go:
>
> **Step 1** — Scan the QR code on the card. The app opens on a welcome screen.
>
> **Step 2** — Pick a username and create your profile to get into the app.
>
> **Step 3** — You'll see 4 tasks on the card. Work through them in order.
>
> **Step 4** — While you do that, I'll be quietly walking around listening and taking notes. If you get properly stuck I'll help, otherwise pretend I'm not here.
>
> **Step 5** — After about 17 minutes I'll call everyone back together. We'll chat through a few questions as a group, then you're done.
>
> A couple of things:
> - **Say what you're thinking out loud** as you go — talk through what you expect, what's confusing, what you're looking for. Everyone talking at once is fine, I'll float around and catch what I can.
> - **All prayers are mock data** — written for testing, not real people.
> - **I'm recording this** so I don't miss anything. Is everyone okay with that?
>
> Any questions? Alright — scan the QR and off you go."

---

## 2. Think-Aloud Session (0:05–0:22)

- [ ] Start timer for **17 minutes**

### Your role

- **Float and listen** — circulate the room
- Spend ~2-3 min per person per circuit, aim for 2-3 full circuits
- On your observation sheet, note **timestamps** + **short descriptions** of key moments (the recording captures full quotes)
- **Don't guide** — if someone asks for help, say: "Just try tapping around and see what happens". Only intervene if they're truly stuck for >60 seconds and visibly frustrated.

### What to watch for, per task

**Task 1 (Find healing prayer + pray for it):**
- Do they notice "Tap a location to pray" on the map?
- Do they tap a map marker, or go straight to the feed?
- In the feed, do they use the "Health" category filter or just scroll?
- Do they find the "Pray for this" button in the drawer?
- Do they see the "Thank you for praying" confirmation?

**Task 2 (Share your own prayer):**
- Do they find the Submit tab in the bottom nav?
- What do they write? (approx. length)
- Do they toggle anonymous on/off?
- Do they change location or category?
- Do they notice the crisis resources section below the form?

**Task 3 (Find a prayer from a different country):**
- Do they use the map (tap markers → "View Prayers")?
- Do they use the feed's country filter dropdown?
- Do they scroll the feed looking at city names?

**Task 4 (Find your prayer in the feed):**
- Do they check the feed first, or profile?
- Feed: do they scroll? Use category filter?
- Profile: do they tap the "Submitted" stat card?

### Handling edge cases

- **Finishes all 4 early:** "Feel free to explore anything you skipped or go back to something that caught your eye"
- **Can't complete a task:** That's useful data — note what they tried and where they got stuck
- **Someone goes silent:** "Just keep talking through it — helps me understand what you're thinking"

### Time cues

| Time | You say |
|------|---------|
| ~0:20 (17 min into session) | "Two more minutes — try to finish up the last task if you can" |
| 0:22 | "Alright, let's pause — put your phones down and let's chat" |

---

## 3. Group Debrief (0:22–0:42)

- [ ] Keep recording
- [ ] Go through the 5 questions
- [ ] **Q1, Q4, Q5** — go around the room, each person answers
- [ ] **Q2, Q3** — open floor, people jump in
- [ ] Capture **one key quote per person** on your debrief sheet as backup

**Q1 (go around):** "What did you think this app was for?"

**Q2 (open floor):** "What frustrated you?"

**Q3 (open floor):** "What would you change first?"

**Q4 (go around):** "Would you use this? Why or why not?"

**Q5 (go around):** "What did you think of getting started — the welcome screen, choosing a username, getting into the app?"

**Facilitation tips:**
- Don't rush silences — give people time to think
- If someone gives a short answer, gently probe: "Tell me more about that" or "What made you feel that way?"
- If one person dominates, redirect: "Let's hear from someone who hasn't shared yet"

---

## 4. Wrap & Reconstruct (0:42–0:45)

- [ ] Say: "That's it — thank you so much. This was incredibly helpful."
- [ ] Collect task cards (or let them keep them)
- [ ] Stop recording
- [ ] **Within 15 minutes of the session ending:**
  - Reconstruct any observations the recording might not catch
  - Note standout moments, recurring patterns, surprises
  - Save recording file with date + session name

---

3. Group findings into themes
